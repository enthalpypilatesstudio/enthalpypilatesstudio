First Visit Orientation

Your first reformer class, made completely calm.

Stepping into a reformer Pilates studio for the first time should feel inspiring, not intimidating. Here is everything you need to know before walking through our doors in Koramangala.

01

What to Wear

Comfortable athletic wear that moves with you. Form-fitting apparel is recommended so instructors can easily check your spinal alignment, pelvis, and ribcage position.

02

Grip Socks Required

For hygiene and non-slip stability on the wooden reformer footbar and leather carriage. You may bring your own or pick up a premium pair at reception upon arrival.

03

Arrive 10 Minutes Early

Your instructor will meet you at reception, show you to your private keyless locker, and give you a personal 5-minute walkthrough of the reformer springs, carriage, and headrest.

04

Hands-On Guidance

Every class has a maximum of 12 reformers. The instructor monitors every movement, offering gentle alignment cues and spring adjustments suited to your body.

The Machine

Meet the Balanced Body Allegro 2.

ENTHALPY features 12 custom Balanced Body Allegro 2 reformers imported directly from California—the gold standard in global clinical and athletic movement.

  • The Gliding Carriage: Rides silently on precision bearings, challenging your core stability.
  • Color-Coded Springs: Provide progressive resistance (yellow = light, blue = medium, red = heavy). Springs assist when you need help and challenge when you need load.
  • Padded Footbar & Soft Straps: Ergonomically shaped for footwork, knee alignment, and shoulder health.
