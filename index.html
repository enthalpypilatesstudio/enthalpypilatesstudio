What we believe

We believe strength is built quietly. One controlled movement, repeated well, until the body learns a better way to hold itself. That is the science of energy, balance and transformation.

The ENTHALPY method
Our philosophy

We treat the body as a system of energy, balance and strength.

Enthalpy is the energy held inside a system. We think about the body the same way: what it stores, how it moves, and how training can change it for good.

Our philosophy

Questions

Before you book.

All FAQs
Do I need any experience?
No. Foundation classes are built for complete beginners and for anyone returning after a break. Your instructor sets up the reformer with you before class starts.
What should I wear?
Fitted, comfortable clothing and grip socks. Loose clothing can catch on the springs and straps.
How do cancellations work?
Cancel free of charge up to [x] hours before class from your account. Late cancellations use the class credit. Full policy on the pricing page.
Is it suitable for men?
Yes. Reformer Pilates builds the deep strength, mobility and control that most gym training leaves out. Our classes are designed for every body.
