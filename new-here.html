01 · What is Pilates?

Strength, built from the inside out.

Pilates is a method of controlled movement that trains deep stabilising muscles, posture and mobility together. Most of our classes use the reformer: a sliding carriage on springs that adds resistance or support, so the same exercise can be made harder or easier for you.

ReformerSpring-loaded carriage, straps and footbar. Guided resistance for every level.
Private 1:1One-on-one reformer sessions customized to your specific posture, injury rehab, or conditioning goals.

Three things people get wrong
"It's only stretching."It is strength work. Springs load your muscles through their full range, and you will feel it the next day.
"It's mainly for women."Joseph Pilates built the method in the 1920s and taught boxers and dancers alike. The reformer suits every body.
"You need to be flexible."Flexibility is a result, not a requirement. Foundation classes start wherever you are.

Beginner questions

Still wondering?

Ask us on WhatsApp
Will I be the only beginner?
Unlikely. Intro and Foundation classes are for beginners only, so everyone in the room is starting out with you.
How many classes a week?
Two a week is a good start. Most people feel a difference in posture and control within a few weeks.
What if I can't keep up?
You set the pace. Your instructor gives an easier option for every exercise, and resting is always fine.
Can I reschedule or cancel?
Yes, up to 12 hours before your scheduled session, directly through WhatsApp or your member account.
