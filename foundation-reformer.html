What you'll do

A guided 50 minutes on the reformer: footwork, bridging, arm work with straps and simple core sequences. Every exercise is taught step by step, with the springs set for you.

Who it's for

Complete beginners, people returning after a break, and anyone who wants to rebuild their technique from the ground up.

What you'll focus on
  • Breath and rib control
  • Neutral spine and pelvis
  • Core activation without strain
  • Safe set-up on the machine

Questions

About this class.

Is this class right for me?
If you are new to the reformer, or haven't trained for a while, yes. If you have practised regularly for three months or more, try Reformer Flow.
How early should I arrive?
10 to 15 minutes before your first class, five minutes after that.
