Getting started

Do I need any experience?
No. Start with an Intro or Foundation class. Your instructor sets up the reformer with you.
Is Pilates suitable for men?
Yes. Every class is designed for every body.
How often should I come?
Two classes a week is a good rhythm to begin with.
What should I wear and bring?
Fitted clothing, grip socks and a water bottle. Towels are provided.

Health & safety

I have an injury. Can I join?
Speak to us first and check with your doctor. A private session may be the best start.
Can I train while pregnant?
With your doctor's approval, and in sessions led by an instructor trained in pre-natal work. Tell us before booking.
Why is there a health declaration?
So your instructor knows how to adapt exercises safely for you. It is kept private.
